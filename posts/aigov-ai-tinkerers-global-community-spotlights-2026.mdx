## AIGov featured in AI Tinkerers' global showcase

On 19 October 2026, **AIGov: Runtime AI Governance** was selected for **AI Tinkerers Community Spotlights**, appearing in the *Top AI Demos #49* global showcase.

In its selection notification, AI Tinkerers explained that the project was **chosen from hundreds of community demos**, based on organizer nominations and audience feedback.

The recognition followed AIGov's presentation at **AI Tinkerers Prague**, where **Zbyněk Nguyen** demonstrated our work on runtime AI governance.

### Why it matters

AIGov is built around a practical question: *Can we reconstruct why an AI system or agent made a particular decision, even after the surrounding models, tools, and policies have changed?*

Our approach focuses on preserving decision-bound evidence, traceability, and reproducible governance assessments so that AI decisions can be reviewed beyond the moment of execution.

Being featured in the international AI Tinkerers community is an encouraging signal that these problems resonate with engineers building and operating real AI systems.
