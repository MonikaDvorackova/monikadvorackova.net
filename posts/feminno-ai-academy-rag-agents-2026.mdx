## FEMINNO AI Academy — Armenia

**Date:** 27 October 2026

**Format:** Online technical workshop

This session explores practical approaches to building **AI assistants**, **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** applications, and **AI agents**.

Topics include choosing between an assistant, RAG, and agentic workflows; grounding answers in retrieved sources; evaluating evidence and handling unsupported claims; and designing systems that remain useful and verifiable when information changes.

The session includes hands-on exercises in which participants build and discuss their own AI systems, identify suitable information sources, and assess how their solutions should behave when evidence is missing.
